Monaco Made Easy

    Monaco, without the climb.

    Monaco climbs about 100 metres from the sea to the top of the town. Around 80 free public lifts, 37 escalators and 8 moving walkways link Monaco, Beausoleil and Cap d’Ail, and Monaco Made Easy shows you how to use them: step-by-step routes, a step-free mode for wheelchairs and pushchairs, the best exit at Monaco Monte-Carlo station, live buses, toilets and pharmacies, sightseeing walks and a guide to restaurants, bars and museums.
